Life World: the model

Described in the ODD protocol (Overview, Design concepts, Details) of Grimm et al. (2006, 2010, 2020). Every value below is the one in the code; file names point to where it lives. FlowGenomics.

1. Purpose and patterns

The model asks how bodies, behaviour and ecological roles evolve when organism traits follow from inherited, recombined and mutated genomes, under an energy budget in a moving environment. The laboratory presets are simplified experimental analogues. Their units and rates are model-specific.

Patterns the model is expected to reproduce, tested by replicated, controlled runs (./run.sh experiment; results on the methods page):

  • Segregation of neutral strains by drift at the edge of an expanding colony, absent in a mixed culture (Hallatschek et al. 2007).
  • Predator and prey both going extinct in a small homogeneous vessel, and prey persisting where a refuge protects them (Gause 1934).
  • Stepwise evolution of resistance across an antibiotic gradient, absent without the drug (Baym et al. 2016).
  • Accumulation of photosynthetic cells toward light, which depends on sensing the gradient.

2. Entities, state variables and scales

World

A 256 by 256 grid of square cells (sim/config.flow), toroidal in the ocean; in a vessel, cells outside the vessel are walls. Each cell holds: terrain (open, ridge, pass), fertility, food (algae standing for primary production and dissolved nutrient, 0 to about 1), detritus, toxin, a light multiplier, and a refuge flag. Fluid velocity lives on a 128 by 128 grid, one fluid cell per 2 by 2 world cells (life/fluid.flow). One tick is the time step; no physical units are assigned.

Global drivers

  • Ocean (life/ocean.flow): phases of current strength (period 6000 ticks), gyre position and upwelling position, each advancing at a constant rate.
  • Sun: a phase with period 2400 ticks; light L = clip((sin φ + 0.25) / 1.25, 0, 1). Laboratory presets use a lamp at constant 0.8 instead.
  • Climate and seasons, inherited from the Genomic World (sim/climate.flow); used in the ocean only.

Organisms

Each organism (life/creature.flow) has: position, heading, energy, age, breeding cooldown, generation, kill count, the core brain's four hidden states, last turn and thrust, a random seed, and a phenotype derived at birth: mass, upkeep, top speed, drag, bite, attack, defence, energy capacity, sensing ranges, photosynthetic capacity, resistance, thermal optimum, breeding threshold, body cells, network size, colour, and two developmental signature words.

Genome

Diploid (life/lgenome.flow). Per haplotype:

PartContents
Fixed, 640 loci on 20 chromosomesMorphology and physiology (128 loci: size, elongation, lobes, pigment, pattern, flagella, cilia, spines, membrane, chlorophyll, resistance, cytostome, eyespot, lipid, developmental rounds, raptorial organelles); core brain weights (432 loci: 72 weights of 6 bits); thermal optimum (16); breeding threshold (12); neutral markers (30); neutral (22).

3. Process overview and scheduling

Each tick, in order (life_step in life/lworld.flow):

  1. Drivers: climate, Ocean and Sun advance; daylight is computed.
  2. Fluid: forcing, friction, semi-Lagrangian advection, pressure projection with walls.
  3. Medium: upwelling sets fertility (ocean, every 16 ticks); food and detritus are advected by the fluid (or food diffuses, in still media); food regrows; detritus is remineralised.
  4. Spatial index of organisms by cell.
  5. Sense and act, all organisms in parallel against a snapshot of the others: senses, brain, turning and thrust, movement with the current, costs, background mortality, ageing.
  6. Spatial index again.
  7. Feeding by cell rows in parallel: grazing, scavenging, photosynthesis.
  8. Predation, serially in slot order.
  9. Reproduction, serially in slot order: sexual or by fission; offspring develop.
  10. Serial transfer, in that preset.
  11. Compaction: the dead are removed and leave detritus.

Every random draw is a hash of (organism seed, tick, purpose), so results do not depend on thread order. The state hash is checked identical at 1 and 8 threads in every mode (tests/life_determinism.flow).

4. Design concepts

  • Basic principles. Energy budgets (intake against upkeep, movement and damage costs) decide survival and reproduction. No selection coefficient is a parameter; selection is measured.
  • Emergence. Trophic roles, body plans, network size, spatial light gathering, resistance, community composition and population dynamics emerge. The revised phototaxis preset also seeds a genetically encoded directional reflex; its evolution has not been demonstrated.
  • Adaptation. No individual adapts; lineages evolve.
  • Objectives. None. Organisms have no fitness function.
  • Learning and prediction. None, except what recurrent neurons can hold over time.
  • Sensing. Food ahead and to each side at the sensing range; nearest prey, threat and possible mate within range, as a side (left or right) weighted by nearness; own energy; left-minus-right illumination at an eyespot (zero when blinded or eyeless); the current across the body; a bias.
  • Interaction. Competition for food and light in a cell; predation within a reach that grows with body radius; mating within that reach.
  • Stochasticity. Mutation, recombination, mate and victim choice, attack success, background mortality, heading noise, offspring placement.
  • Collectives. None are modelled; groups and forms are observed only.
  • Observation. A census every 64 ticks: forms (body plans hashed), trophic roles, energy by source, means of traits, program and network size, selection differentials (parents minus all) on speed, width and network size.

5. Initialisation

Founders (3000 in the ocean; per preset otherwise) are copies of one genotype: in multicellular modes a core with a mouth in front and a motor behind; in morphology mode a small flagellate with a cytostome. Their brains turn toward food and slow over it; the core's hidden layer is silent and there are no evolved neurons. Each founder carries a Poisson number (mean 3) of random mutations in its brain loci or developmental program, as standing variation. Founders are split into marker groups by nearest group centre, or at random where a preset needs the groups well mixed.

6. Input data

The Life World has no empirical input data. Terrain comes from the Genomic World's seeded generator (two ridges with passes); vessels are drawn geometrically. The separate LTEE analysis uses published competition measurements to estimate a descriptive fitness trajectory. It does not calibrate Life World's organism rules.

Archived LTEE competition data and binned means. The fit is reproducible with python3 analysis/ltee_fit.py. Methods and source details are in data/README.md.

7. Submodels

Development

The expressed program takes, gene by gene, the larger allele value of each field (growth dominant over none). A body starts as one cell of identity 0 at the origin. For up to 1 + (dosage of 12 round loci) rounds, every cell in the upper half (y ≤ 0) whose identity's timer has not expired grows, in each direction whose grow bit is set, a cell of the target identity (target modulo program length) into an empty position still in the upper half. Growth stops when a round adds nothing. The upper half is then mirrored. 2D bodies use four directions, 3D bodies six.

Phenotype

Multicellular modes: mass and upkeep are sums over cells (mass 1, armour 2, eye 0.5, photosynthetic 0.7; upkeep 1, motor 1.5, eye and mouth 1.2, armour 0.8, store and photosynthetic 0.5). Drag D = 0.35 + 0.175 (F − 1) + 0.03 N with F the frontal area (distinct (y, z) columns) and N cells. Top speed min(2.5, 0.4 · motors · f / D). Bite 0.06 · mouths · f / (1 + spikes). Attack spikes · f; defence armour · f + 0.5 spikes. Energy capacity 1 + 1.5 mass + 3 · stores · f. Factors f are 1 in body-plan mode and 0.5 + trait in combined mode, with extra upkeep 0.6 per unit above 1.

Morphology mode: radius r = 0.5 + 2 · size, aspect 1 + 2.5 · elongation; thrust from flagella and cilia; drag from width, area and lobes; bite 0.12 (0.3 + r) · cytostome / (1 + 3 · raptorial); attack 3 · raptorial · r; defence 0.3 r + 1.8 · membrane + 0.25 · spines; photosynthetic capacity 0.6 · chlorophyll · area; upkeep sums the cost of each structure.

All modes: resistance adds 1.5 × resistance to upkeep. Breeding threshold (0.35 + 0.5 t) · capacity with t the threshold dosage fraction.

Brain

Core: h_j ← tanh(Σ_i w_ji x_i + r_j h_j); turning clip(Σ w x + Σ w h + E_turn, −1, 1); thrust logistic(Σ w x + Σ w h + E_thrust). Evolved neurons: s_k ← act_k(b_k + a_k s_k + Σ_c w_c src_c), all updated together from senses, the core's new state and their own previous values; E_turn and E_thrust are the sums of their output weights times their new values. Weights (v − 31.5) / 8 for 6-bit v.

Movement and costs

Heading changes by 0.6 · turn + U(−0.05, 0.05). Position moves by thrust · top speed · viscosity along the heading plus the fluid velocity; a move into a wall is refused and the heading reversed. Cost per tick 0.004 · upkeep + 0.0005 · expressed evolved neurons + 0.0016 · D · v² + thermal cost 0.03 · mass · (T − T_opt)² (ocean) + toxin harm 0.12 · max(0, toxin − 4 · resistance). Background mortality 0.002 per tick; maximum age 900.

Feeding

Per cell, organisms in index order: a mouth takes up to its bite of food, then the rest of its bite of detritus at efficiency 0.7. Photosynthesis yields 0.06 · L · light map / (1 + 0.6 (food + detritus)) · capacity · share, where share is min(1, 6 / total capacity in the cell), and consumes 0.25 food per unit of energy. Energy is capped at capacity.

Predation

An organism with a mouth and attack pays 0.004 and picks uniformly one organism within reach 1 + floor(r / 2) (at most 4) cells, outside any refuge, with defence below its attack. It succeeds with probability a / (a + d + 1), doing damage 0.4 a and gaining 0.6 of it; a kill adds half the victim's mass.

Reproduction

An organism of age 25 or more, off cooldown, with energy at or above its threshold, seeks a mate with probability sex (1 in the ocean, 0.1 for protists, 0 for bacteria): one uniformly among those within reach, mature, off cooldown, with energy above half their threshold, and with developmental signatures within 10 bits. Otherwise it divides alone. Parents pay 0.55 and 0.15 of their thresholds (0.7 for fission); the child receives 0.8 × payments − 0.08 × its mass, or is stillborn if that is not positive. Cooldown 20 ticks.

Inheritance

Fixed loci: one gamete per parent by Haldane meiosis (1 Morgan per chromosome) and point mutation at 0.0008 per locus. Gene lists: at most one crossover (probability 0.4) whose join is misaligned by one gene with probability 0.08; point mutation at 0.0008 per bit; duplication 0.004 and deletion 0.004 per gamete for the program, 0.006 each for neurons; insertion of a near-silent random neuron 0.02 per gamete.

Medium

Food regrows F ← F + 0.012 · season · regrow · (fertility − F); detritus remineralises at 0.004 per tick into food; in still media food diffuses with coefficient 0.12 and no flux into walls. Deaths deposit 0.5 × mass (half that after a kill) plus remaining energy as detritus.

Fluid

Stam's stable fluids: forcing from a stream function ψ = F sin(kX + g_x) sin(kY + g_y) with k = 2 periods, amplitude 0.018 × strength × habitat flow; friction 0.02 per tick; semi-Lagrangian advection; 20 Jacobi iterations of a pressure projection whose Laplacian is the exact composition of the discrete divergence and gradient, wall rules included.

9. Limits

  • Energy, time and distance use model units. Rates were chosen so behaviour appears within minutes of running. They are not fitted to an organism.
  • The world is two-dimensional; 3D bodies swim in a plane.
  • Body development uses scratch grids of 64 by 64 in 2D or 32 by 32 by 32 in 3D, a 32,768-cell working list, and a 31-cell or 15-cell extent from the origin. Programs hold up to 48 genes and networks up to 32 neurons per haplotype.
  • Algae are a field; bacteria that remineralise detritus are implicit.
  • The LTEE preset represents a 1% random bottleneck and food reset every 600 model ticks. It does not simulate the 1:100 dilution, DM25 recipe, or growth during the 24-hour cycle. The tick-to-day mapping, cell rates and fitness parameters remain uncalibrated.
  • The archived LTEE measurements have a separate descriptive power-law fit. This fit is an empirical reference and does not calibrate Life World.
